About Me

I'm a Senior System Administrator with over a decade of experience in managing enterprise-grade Linux and Windows environments, automating infrastructure, and supporting mission-critical systems. At Siemens, I help operate and maintain SCADA infrastructure (Spectrum Power 7) across the Nordic region. My focus is on Linux system administration, automation with Ansible, monitoring with Zabbix/Nagios, and database support (Oracle). Previously at Atlassian, I provided technical support for Confluence to some of the world's most recognized companies, including Apple and Disney. That experience sharpened my communication skills and gave me a deep understanding of enterprise collaboration tools and customer success at scale. I'm passionate about technology that enables stability and efficiency, and I thrive in environments that combine technical depth with international collaboration.
