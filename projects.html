GEF Biogás Brasil · Brazil

Municipal Solid Waste Treatment Route Simulator

A decision-support tool that helps municipalities and inter-municipal consortia plan regional solid waste treatment. It models how municipalities can be grouped, compares treatment routes and calculates final costs, so public managers can evaluate options before committing to infrastructure investment.

Programme
GEF Biogás Brasil, led by Brazil's Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation (MCTI), implemented by UNIDO and funded by the Global Environment Facility.
Partners
CIBiogás, the Federal University of Santa Catarina (UFSC) and the Greater Florianópolis municipal association.
My role
Sole developer. Designed and built the entire application, implementing the calculation model defined by the project's technical specialists.
Technology
Python, Microsoft Excel

What the tool evaluates

  • Five treatment technologies: biodigestion for electricity, biodigestion for biomethane, biodrying, composting and incineration.
  • Recycling rates and landfill diversion.
  • Direct and indirect job creation.
  • Greenhouse gas emission reductions.
  • Financial impact on end consumers.
